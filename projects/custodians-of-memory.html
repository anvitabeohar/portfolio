Custodians of Memory

An immersive VR experience reimagining iconic paintings as explorable 3D worlds

Role: Project Manager & Gameplay Developer Team: 5 members, interdisciplinary Platform: Unity (VR)

Project Overview

The problem Museum-goers typically engage with art passively viewing paintings behind glass, at a distance, with limited context about the story, technique, or culture behind the work. This creates a barrier between the artwork and the audience, especially for younger or less traditionally "museum-going" visitors.

The goal Design and build a VR experience that lets players step inside iconic paintings, transforming static 2D artworks into interactive, explorable 3D environments - giving audiences an active, embodied way to discover the stories, techniques, and cultural contexts behind the art.

Paintings featured

  • Damayanti - Raja Ravi Varma (India)
  • Virginia's Fish - Leonora Carrington (Mexico)
  • The Night Revels of Han Xizai - Gu Hongzhong (China)

My responsibilities

  • Gameplay systems development (locomotion, interaction, transitions, optimization)
  • Project management across a 5-person team
  • Integration "gatekeeper" for the shared Unity project
  • Branding and showcase logistics

The Challenge

Moving beyond passive viewing meant solving three distinct problems at once:

  1. Translation: converting flat, historical 2D paintings into spatially coherent, explorable 3D environments without losing artistic authenticity.
  2. Interaction design: building intuitive systems (locomotion, object grabbing, cues) that felt natural in VR for first-time users.
  3. Team coordination — managing a 5-person team with different skills, schedules, and tools under a tight deadline, including a Unity project structure that made parallel collaboration difficult.

Design & Development Process

1. Research & Selection

Evaluated candidate paintings against three criteria: visual richness, narrative depth, and feasibility as a 3D environment. This grounded the final selection of three culturally distinct, visually strong pieces from India, Mexico, and China.

2. Design & Implementation

  • Locomotion framework - built an anchor-based teleportation system alongside continuous movement, giving players a comfortable, flexible way to navigate each scene.
  • Core interactions - object grabbing, scene transitions, and haptic feedback cues designed to feel intuitive without instructions.
  • Discovery-first cues - subtle interaction prompts introduced to guide players without breaking the sense of exploration.
  • Scene transitions - particle-based loading screens for smooth movement between paintings.
  • Audio design - tailored soundscapes built for each painting's specific cultural context.
  • Performance optimization - baked lighting and unlit textures used to keep heavy assets performant without compromising visual authenticity.

3. Collaboration & Workflow

GitHub proved unworkable for large Unity binaries early on. In response, the team pivoted to a sequential integration model using Google Drive and WeTransfer, with me acting as the sole integrator into the master Unity project. This was paired with weekly supervisor meetings and internal check-ins to keep a 5-person team with varying schedules aligned.

Outcome

  • A functioning VR prototype letting players explore three culturally significant paintings in immersive 3D.
  • Showcase visitors described the experience as "magical" and "immersive," and expressed interest in seeing more artworks brought into VR.
  • Positive feedback on the concept's potential for museum and educational use.
  • Constructive feedback shaped concrete next-iteration plans: freer teleportation and AI-driven character interactions.

Impact & Learnings

Technical Deepened fluency in Unity VR workflows, C# scripting for scene transitions, asset/performance optimization, and haptic interaction design.

Managerial Learned to coordinate an interdisciplinary team through workflow breakdowns, manage branding and showcase logistics end-to-end, and balance a leadership role with hands-on development.

Creative Developed a sharper sense for preserving artistic authenticity while layering in interactivity treating the original artwork as source material to be respected, not just a backdrop.

Next Steps

  • Expand to additional artworks and cultures beyond the initial three.
  • Implement conversational AI for characters living inside each painting.
  • Explore partnerships with museums and cultural institutions to develop the project into a deployable educational exhibit.
